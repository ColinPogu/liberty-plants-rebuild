Come say hello at the market

You can find our flowers at the Chestnut Hill Farmer's Market on Saturdays. We are there most Saturdays through the growing season, with fresh bunches, bulk buckets, and florist pick-ups.

Prefer to plan ahead? Pre-ordered bulk buckets and pick-ups in Trappe are available by appointment. Follow along on Instagram for the week to week schedule.
